# Visual reference: Storrito video packaging

Source: https://www.youtube.com/@storrito/videos

Inspected: the first 12 public uploads returned by the channel's Videos tab in this development session, using each video's actual thumbnail image. This is a sample, not a claim that the entire channel has one style. Reference images are not bundled in the reusable skill.

## What repeats

A recognizable tutorial series uses:
- saturated purple/pink/orange gradients;
- very large bold white sans-serif headlines on the left, often three lines, sometimes uppercase;
- a desktop screenshot/monitor and/or phone or presenter on the right;
- a simple white directional arrow linking the promise to the shown UI;
- occasional original yellow Storrito mascot and small logo/web address;
- a clear tutorial action, not an abstract brand slogan.

Examples in the inspected set:
- `GsAphteSixA` — Post To TikTik Straight From Your Desktop (thumbnail says “TikTok From Desktop!”).
- `KO7-b78JWBY` — Cancel Scheduled IG Stories in Bulk with Storrito!
- `FhLCWxkXtw8` — Schedule Instagram Stories in Bulk with Storrito!
- `MVa32SQ7NKE` — Manage Layers in your Instagram Story Designs with the Storrito Editor.
- `1evfkw_JPjg` — How to add Clickable Stickers to IG Stories from your Desktop!
- `Yd2X-W9X7SM` — Add the Link Sticker to Instagram Stories from your PC with Storrito!

## Other styles in the same sample

- `fvnj1ULYcUE` and `v0aiEsXuOx0`: photographic person, bright pastel ground/brush shapes and black caption strips with white type.
- `c-9aJYQRo1c`: a topic-specific visual metaphor, curved title, composited imagery.
- `jIf8cPa4o08`: blurred website background and a dark title strip.
- `RpzPzakx5xg` and `ihk7_Ha4Q2E`: primarily application/video screenshots; not examples of the branded large-type series.

Do not describe all 12 as one uniform design system. For a newly requested style, ask which series they mean if the distinction matters.

## Transfer, don't clone

The default skill transfers **hierarchy and semantic structure**: prominent promise + relevant video subject + minimal directional cue. It does not copy thumbnails pixel-for-pixel, use another brand's logo, reuse a presenter's face without a supplied/authorized source, or invent platform/product claims.

The palette, fonts, brand voice and logo come from the job's actual `DESIGN.md`. The yellow mascot and Instagram-colored gradient are not universal defaults. A matching intro is a new animated interpretation of that thumbnail layout; the channel thumbnails alone do not establish what Storrito's original intros look like.
