# Layouts

Patterns for a vertical frame (a Reel, a Short, a TikTok, a Story), each placed by the zones of the
platforms it goes to (`zones.mjs zones … --json`): `centred` (the safe box even about the frame's
middle), `wide` (clear across the frame above the buttons), the safe box's bottom (where captions
end). The numbers are always the tool's, never written into a layout. Pick the pattern by the
content, then adapt it: these are starting points, not templates. The pictures of all of them (for
people, and to show the person when they choose a style): [../examples/](../examples/).

The rules every pattern keeps are SKILL.md's "Laying out a frame". In short: text centred on the
frame and at most `centred` wide; something wide in `wide`; captions end on the safe box's bottom;
faces in the upper third, never under text; under what the apps cover only ground.

## Talking heads

| pattern | where |
| --- | --- |
| **Talking head** | The face in the upper third (eyes at about a third of the height); captions end on the safe box's bottom, over the chest. |
| **Hook + talking head** | The hook (a card or bold words, 3–8 words) in `wide`, at its top; the face below it; captions at the bottom. The hook stays while the first sentence is said, or the whole video. |
| **Series part** | A small badge ("Part 2/5") at the left edge of the safe box's top; the series' name centred under it; face and captions as above. |
| **Reply to a comment** | The comment as a white card in `wide` (who wrote it, small, above the comment); face and captions below. |
| **Podcast clip** | The show's name small at the top of `centred`; the face; a name tag (name, role) beside the face or under the chin, at the left edge of `centred`; an optional waveform just above the captions. |
| **Two people stacked** | Each in one half, the faces in the upper part of their half and inside the safe box; captions on the seam between the halves (above the buttons' column when it starts lower); names at the left edge beside each face, never on a face. |
| **Reaction** | What is reacted to in the top half (a label "Original" on it), the face in the bottom half, inside the safe box; captions on the seam. |
| **Green screen** | A screenshot or article as a card in `wide`; the person cut out below it, the face inside the safe box; captions between the card and the head, never on the face. |
| **Street interview** | The question as a card in `wide`; the person with the microphone below; the answer as captions at the bottom. |
| **End card** | The face high; the background darkened towards the bottom; the call ("Follow for more", big) and the handle (a pill) centred above the safe box's bottom. |

## Text

| pattern | where |
| --- | --- |
| **Text hero** | One statement, 3–8 words, as big as `centred` allows, centred in the frame; one or two words in a second colour. |
| **POV / caption over b-roll** | One sentence in `wide`, at its top, over the picture; the picture carries the rest. |
| **Meme top and bottom** | Top line at the top of `centred`, bottom line ending on the safe box's bottom; both outlined. |
| **Kinetic type** | One big word in the middle, the small words around it in a column; never two phrases at once. |
| **Chat / DM** | Bubbles in `centred`, the other's on the left, one's own on the right, at most 80 % of the width each; the name above. |
| **Post screenshot** | The post as a card centred in `centred`, vertically in the middle. |
| **Notes / checklist** | A note in `centred`: the title, then 3–5 items with ticks, the done ones struck through. |
| **Myth vs. fact** | Two cards stacked in `centred`: the myth (struck through), the fact below it. |
| **This or that** | Two pictures side by side in `centred`, a "VS" disc on the seam; the question as captions. |
| **Ranking / tier list** | The title, then rows (S, A, B, C) with chips, in `centred`. |

## Information

| pattern | where |
| --- | --- |
| **Big number** | The number as big as `wide` allows, what it means under it; captions at the bottom. |
| **List** | A title, then 3–5 numbered items as cards, in `centred`, vertically centred. |
| **Chart** | Title, the bars or the line, the source small under it, in `centred`; the value that matters in the accent colour. |
| **Timeline** | A vertical line with 3–5 points (year bold, one line of text) in `centred`. |
| **Tutorial step** | A badge "Step 2 of 5" at the top; the phone or screen in the middle with the place to tap ringed; the instruction as captions. |
| **Screencast + face** | The screen across `wide`; the face as a circle under it at the left edge of `centred`; captions at the bottom. |
| **News / announcement** | A picture; a label ("NEW") and the headline, big, ending near the safe box's bottom; the date under it. |

## Products and trades

| pattern | where |
| --- | --- |
| **Product** | The product in the upper half, in `wide`; the price big under it, a badge ("–30 % sugar"); "Link in bio" as a pill above the safe box's bottom (a Story: a link sticker). |
| **Product with callouts** | The product in the middle; 2–4 callouts with lines pointing at it, alternately at the left and the right edge of `centred`. |
| **Recipe** | The dish in the upper half; a card in the lower half with the name, chips (time, kcal, vegan) and the ingredients in two columns. |
| **Property** | The house; a card at the top of `centred` with the price (big), the kind and place, chips (rooms, m², plot). |
| **Workout** | The exercise's name and the reps at the top; a timer ring in the middle; the cue as captions. |
| **Travel** | The place's name huge in `wide`, the country and days small above it; the landscape behind; captions at the bottom. |
| **Outfit** | The person full length; price tags at the left and right edge of `centred`, each pointing at its piece; the title above the head. |
| **Before / after** | Top half before, bottom half after, each with a label at its top. |
| **Behind the scenes** | A handwritten note in `centred`, at an angle; a small badge "BTS"; captions at the bottom. |
| **Quote** | The quote big, in a serif, in `wide`; the person (portrait in a circle, name, role) above the safe box's bottom. |

## Stories with stickers

Storrito adds Instagram's stickers to any Story (SKILL.md, "Stickers on Stories"). A Story has no buttons' column: `centred` is the whole safe box.

| pattern | where |
| --- | --- |
| **Poll** | The question as text at the top of the safe box over the picture; the poll sticker in the lower middle, clear of the subject. |
| **Article teaser + link** | The article's picture at the top, a label ("NEW ON THE BLOG") and the headline under it; the link sticker centred above the safe box's bottom. |
| **Event + countdown** | Kind, title and date at the top; the countdown sticker in the middle; the link sticker (sign up) at the bottom. |
| **Discount code** | The discount huge, what it is for, the code in a dashed box; the link sticker at the bottom. |
| **Giveaway** | "GIVEAWAY", the prize, the steps (follow, like, tag) and the closing date, all centred; no sticker needed (a mention sticker for the account works). |
| **Question box** | The face; the question sticker at the top of the safe box, clear of the face. |
| **Review + slider** | Stars, the review, who wrote it; an emoji slider below, above the safe box's bottom. |
| **Job ad** | "WE'RE HIRING", the role big, 3 benefits; the link sticker (apply) at the bottom. |
| **Opening / place** | A map with a pin; a card with "NEW OPENING", the place and the date; a location sticker at the top. |
