# Which layout fits which situation

The situations are footage-kind's (its `references/situations.md`: P1, G1, B2, …). For the eleven
that were laid out so far: the layouts that fit, the part of a video each is for, and what
`place` gives for it. A layout changes with what carries the moment, part by part, not once per
video: run `place` for each layout a video uses.

Every number here is marked. **240 Shorts**: measured on published videos (below). **measured**:
seen on our own footage or stills, with what it was seen on. **computed**: the solver's own, on
made-up faces; nobody looked at pictures of it. **sketch**: drawn to scale in the catalogue of
layouts (27 layouts, 2026-11-16) and never tested. **from** a skill: that skill's own number.

## What published Shorts do (240 Shorts)

240 YouTube Shorts of 2026-11-17, 60 each from lists of talking heads, podcast clips, screens with
a face and explainers, five stills of each read by a model. The boxes of faces, captions and
screens were checked by eye on 28 videos and are good; which situation a video is and which
layout a frame has are the model's reading and were not verified. They are the most viewed
videos of hand-picked German and English channels, on one platform.

**These figures say what is usual, not what works.** Nothing separates the top third of the
videos from the bottom third by views per follower: 5 of 69 comparisons fall under p = 0.05, and
3.5 would by chance.

- **A face is about a quarter of the frame's height.** Hairline to chin 26 % in talking heads (49
  videos; middle half 23–34 %) and 29 % in podcast clips (60 videos; 23–34 %). The chin lies at
  half height (middle half 43–57 %), the eye line near 32 % (estimated from the box, not
  measured). The lower half of the frame belongs to the chest and the text.
- **Captions sit below the chin, on the chest**: in 505 of 576 frames with a face and a caption
  (88 %); above the head in 50 (9 %), on the face in 19 (3 %). Their middle lies at 60–75 % of
  the frame's height and stays there within a video (in 131 of 177 videos it moves less than
  3 % of the height), whatever the face does.
- **When the chin lies lower than 67 % of the height** (89 frames of 35 videos: rare) the
  captions are above the head in 48 %, below in 36 %, on the face in 13 %. Most of those above
  are stacked layouts with the captions on the seam. In a full frame with one face creators
  avoid the case by framing: the chin is lower than 67 % in 48 of 623 frames. Of the 32 such
  frames with captions these stay under the chin in 17, run over the face in 8 and go above the
  head in 6 (from 3 videos).
- **Captions are small and short**: a line is 4.0 % of the frame's height (188 videos; middle
  half 3.2–4.9 %), the block half the frame wide, 77 % of the blocks one line, three words in the
  median.
- **A hook text** stands in the frame at the first second in 85 of 240 videos (35 %; 31–43 %,
  as the model mixed hook and first caption up in 2 of 20 checked videos). Its middle lies at
  53 % of the height (middle half 26–65 %): 37 of the 85 in the lower middle, where the captions
  stand afterwards, 26 at the top. The block is 12.4 % of the height tall and 72 % of the width
  wide, six words. In 64 of 85 it is gone by the next still. With a caption in the same frame
  (29 videos) it stands above it in 24.
- **A screen with a face** (33 videos of 18 channels: few). Of 53 frames that show both, the face
  is below the screen in 35 (9 videos), 18 % of the frame's height tall; above it in 9; a small
  picture in or at the screen in 9. The screen takes 59 % of the frame, nearly always its full
  width. Screen and face take turns more than they share the frame: 41 frames show the screen
  alone, 52 the face alone.
- **Two people** (60 podcast clips, sorted by eye): a cut between the speakers in 24 videos, one
  person throughout in 18, both at once in 16: one shot of both 8, stacked 7, side by side 1. In
  1,200 frames no two pictures stand side by side over the frame's whole height.

## What holds for all

- What carries the moment gets the largest area (sketch).
- Faces in the safe box, never text on a face (platform-zones); a face about a quarter of the
  frame tall with the eyes a third down (240 Shorts).
- Captions stand below the chin and end on the safe box's bottom; with two parts one above the
  other, on the seam (platform-zones; 240 Shorts).
- **A take filmed so close that the face is 47 % of the frame's height with the chin at 77 % is
  unusual.** Text above the head is the answer for such a take, not the general rule, and the
  first advice is to film with more distance. (The upright take that was laid out by hand is such
  a take: below.)
- No empty band for long: a source that does not fill the frame gets a second area (text, a
  person, a magnified part). The blurred copy as ground is for short inserts (sketch).
- 16:9 uses the width: what stands one above the other in 9:16 stands side by side, the main thing
  on two thirds (sketch; no published 16:9 video was measured).

## One person speaks to the camera

| situation | layout | when | how | from `place` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 upright | The person alone | every part in which someone only speaks | 9:16: the take fills the frame, captions below the chin. 16:9: the take in the middle, the sides ground, the captions over its bottom; or as a tall panel on the left, the words beside it | 9:16 `full`, `zoom` or `inset`; 16:9 `inset` (middle) or `panel` |
| P1 | Hook in the middle | the first second or two; then the captions take its place | 9:16: a short text of about six words on the chest, above the captions when both show | `--text hook`: a text below the chin, else above the head |
| P1 | Hook card at the top | a hook that stays: a reply to a comment, a part of a series | 9:16: a card of 3–8 words in the wide stretch, the face under it. 16:9: big lines beside the panel, captions small under them | `--text hook:card`; in 16:9 big lines |
| P2 wide | Cropped close | from about 2.7K material | 9:16: a crop around the face, 31.6 % of the source's width. 16:9: the source as it is, one caption line at the bottom | `crop 9:16`; 16:9 `full` |
| P2 | Square plus text | 1080-line material and less, or when keywords stand beside the speaking | 9:16: a square across the frame, captions under it on calm ground. 16:9: the person on a third, the keyword on the free side | `crop 1:1`, `crop 4:5`, `crop band` |
| P4 speaker and material | Material on top, person below | the person speaks about the material for a while and stays visible | 9:16: material in the wide stretch, the person under it, captions on the seam. 16:9: material on two thirds, the person as a panel | material made for the moment (a card, a quote): `beside` (`beside-above`; 16:9 `beside-column`, unmeasured); footage or a screen recording as material: `--region` with the speaker as the webcam, `screen-stacked` |
| P4 | Material fills, person in a circle | the material carries many sentences | circle 22 % of the frame's width, left above the captions (9:16); 20 % of the height, top right (16:9) | a screen or picture as `--region` with the speaker as the webcam |
| P4 | Material beside the speaker | b-roll of 2–4 s (a number, a quote, a screenshot) while the speaker stays in the picture | 9:16, 4:5, 1:1: the material above, the speaker's panel below, captions on the seam; else the speaker as a circle. 16:9: the speaker as a column on a third, the material on two | `beside` (below) |
| P4 | Insert | proof of 2–5 seconds, the voice goes on | upright material fills; wide material as a band over a blurred copy in 9:16, hence short | `inset`, `crop band` |

- **Which hook is which.** The text in the middle is what creators publish (240 Shorts): short,
  on the chest, gone after the first seconds. The card across the top is platform-zones' pattern
  "Hook + talking head", a sketch; 26 of the 85 measured hooks do stand at the top. `--text hook`
  tries the first and takes the card only where the text finds no room.
- **The close take as sketched fails in 9:16** (measured: one still of an upright phone take, the
  face 47 % of the height, the chin at 77 %; platform-zones' check, usual platforms, 2026-11-16).
  Captions at the safe box's bottom lay on the face, the chin under TikTok's and Facebook Reels'
  text, the cheek under Shorts' buttons. What passed: the picture zoomed in 7 % and moved up and
  to the left, the text above the head; one caption line fits there, or a hook card at 66 px, not
  both. Both need the picture at about 82 %. For Instagram Reels alone the take passed unchanged:
  name the platform when it is known.
- **In the video the margin was 8 px** (measured: four test videos of 7.7 s from the same take,
  2026-11-17; 36 stills checked, none failing). The top of the hair moved between y 448 and 470
  of the frame, the caption box above it ended at 440. A face read at one moment has no reserve:
  read it at several.
- **The two 16:9 layouts of an upright take passed every check and looked weak as videos**
  (measured: the same four videos, looked at by people). The big words beside the panel were
  restless, and after the hook the area beside the panel stood empty. Take them only when 16:9 is
  asked for, and say that an upright take suits it badly.
- **P2's sharpness** (measured: stills of one 1280×720 interview, judged by people, 2026-11-17).
  The close crop at 2.67× was too soft to publish, the 4:5 crop at 1.875× mildly soft but fine,
  the square at 1.5× crisp, with the person small (the face a quarter of the width). Nothing
  between 1.875× and 2.67× was judged: the solver's limit of 2× is a guess between them.
  "From about 2.7K" and "emphasis by zooming in 15–30 %" are sketch.
- The person on a third in 16:9 took 2.02× on that still, as the person sat in the source's
  middle, and the keyword was readable only on a darkened ground (measured, same still). The
  solver knows neither: it has no third and sees no ground.

## Several people speak

| situation | layout | when | how | from `place` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 two in one picture | Cut to the speaker | the usual: one person speaks, the other is not in the frame | 9:16: a crop on the speaker's face, a pan of 0.4 s when the speaker changes. 16:9: both stay | `--speaker N`: `crop` on that face; 16:9 `full` |
| G1 | Stacked | quick exchange, laughter, contradiction: both faces count | 9:16: each person in one half, captions on the seam | `stacked`; in 16:9 the picture stays whole |
| G2 one file per person | Cut | the usual; cut where the speaker changes, no shot under about 1.5 s | the speaker's camera, cropped as P2 | `place` on that file alone |
| G2 | Split | exchange and reactions | 9:16: two cameras one above the other, captions on the seam. 16:9: side by side | two `--source`: `stacked`, `side-by-side` |

- **A crop on whoever speaks is more common than stacking** (240 Shorts: a cut between speakers
  in 24 of 60 podcast clips, stacked in 7, one shot of both in 8). `place` cannot know who
  speaks: without `--speaker` it puts the candidates that show both first and lists the crops
  behind them. Name the speaker for each part.
- "1.5 s" is sketch; the pan of 0.4 s and the face at 32 % of the height are from clips. Stacked
  and side by side were run only on drawn faces (computed): never on a recording of two people.
  Name tags are not placed.

## Material beside the speaker (`beside`)

What it builds rests on figures from screens beside a face, not from cards over a talking head
(240 Shorts: 53 frames that show a screen and a face, from 33 videos of 18 channels): the face
below the screen in 35 (66 %), 18.0 % of the frame's height tall (middle half 17.1–19.4 %); above
it in 9; a small picture in or at the screen in 9 (14.2 %; in the frames whose layout is a
picture in a picture the median face is 12.0 %, 12 frames); beside it in none, and two pictures
side by side over the frame's whole height in none of 1,200 frames. The screen of a stacked frame
takes about half the frame (51 % of its area), nearly always the full width, and the captions of
stacked frames stand on the seam. In a quarter of the frames with a screen (41 of 165) no face
shows at all. **Usual, not shown to work**, and none of it for 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9.

| frame | first | then | marked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9:16 | material above, speaker's panel below, the face towards 18 %, captions on the seam | the speaker as a circle bottom left | 240 Shorts (the order and the 18 %); the circle's size from screencast-cut |
| 4:5, 1:1 | the same, while the panel keeps the face at 12 % or more | the circle top right; first where the material needs more height | computed: the frame's height carries the panel (with one caption line and the face at 12 %, whatever the take: a field up to 828×662 in 9:16, 972×775 in 4:5, 810×549 in 1:1) |
| 16:9 | the speaker as a column on a third, on the side they look to the material from | the panel below only where the material keeps its field, then the circle | sketch: nothing measured; the look is a guess from the face's place in the source |

- **The 12 %** below which a panel gives way to the circle is the median face of the picture in a
  picture (240 Shorts). That a face of 11 % in a panel is worse than one of 6 % in a circle is
  not shown: it only keeps the panel from shrinking the speaker past what published videos show.
- **A card is laid out into its field** (computed): the field is the room above the seam,
  inside the safe box or the wide stretch above the buttons, inside every feed's crop where it
  keeps its size. A screenshot or a clip of fixed size gets the largest box of its shape.
- **Measured on two stills** (2026-11-18, `layout-proben/beside/`, a made number card): the card
  reads in every field, the checker passes all 16 candidates. The panel in 9:16 and 4:5 looks
  like the published layout. Weak: the circle's face (6–11 % of the height) is small; in 16:9 the
  panel below the card leaves a banner of a card and ground beside the speaker; an upright take
  with a large face fills no panel's width in 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9 and stands on ground. Never built
  into a video.

## The screen is the subject

| situation | layout | when | how | from `place` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 desktop alone | Zoom on the spot | the usual, once it is clear where something happens | the part that matters in the safe box, the rest of the screen running on under the apps' bars, never under the captions | `screen-zoom`, `screen-fit` |
| B1 | Whole screen | one or two seconds to find one's way: at the start, when the application changes | 9:16: the screen across the frame, nothing readable, hence short | `screen-whole` |
| B1 | Crop plus step text | a recording without a voice, or a guide in steps | 9:16: the crop at the top with a ring, the step under it. 16:9: the screen on two thirds, the step on the right | the crop: `screen-zoom`; the step text is not placed |
| B2 desktop and webcam | Screen on top, face below | the usual in published Shorts, when both show | 9:16: the screen across the upper part, the face in a panel under it, about 18 % of the frame's height tall | `screen-stacked` (upright frames) |
| B2 | Screen with circle | the screen carries, the person only stays present | circle 22 % of the width, left above the captions (9:16); 20 % of the height, top right (16:9) | `--region` with a second `--source` and its `--face` |
| B2 | Face large | first sentence, last sentence, a personal remark | the webcam fills the frame, enlarged 1.25× at most | `place` on the webcam's file alone |
| B4 phone alone | Phone whole | the opening: it is the real app on a real phone | 9:16: the phone in the middle of the safe box, about half the width | `screen-whole` |
| B4 | Phone fills the frame | as soon as someone taps and reads | 9:16: the screen across the frame, its status bar under the apps' bars | `screen-zoom`, `screen-fit` |
| B5 phone and speaker | Phone whole, speaker beside | the person introduces | 9:16: the circle left above the captions, the phone beside it | `screen-whole` with a webcam |
| B5 | Phone fills, circle in a free corner | as soon as someone taps | the circle moves to another corner when the part that matters is there | `screen-zoom` with a webcam |

- **The face below the screen is the common layout, the small circle the exception** (240
  Shorts: below in 35 of 53 frames, a small picture in or at the screen in 9; from 33 videos of
  18 channels, so a few channels carry it). `screen-stacked` builds it in upright frames: the
  screen in the upper half, the face towards 18 % of the frame's height. It was run once on a real
  desktop recording with a webcam file (2026-11-18), the face 13 % at the soft limit of a 720p
  webcam; nobody judged its pictures against the circle yet.
- The circle's sizes (22 % of the width in 9:16, 16 % of the short side in 4:5 and 1:1, 20 % in
  16:9), the least share of the screen (38 % of its width) and the 1.25× of "Face large" are from
  screencast-cut. The times and "about half the width" are sketch.
- What reads is a guess: a capital letter of the screen's smallest text 24 px tall in a frame
  1080 px wide (`--readable`). Nobody looked at a screen recording enlarged 1.5–2×; the limits
  come from a person's still.
- Every screen case is computed on a drawn surface: never run on a screen recording, and a phone's
  upright screen not at all.

## No picture of its own

O1 (a voice alone: the words as the picture, a cover with a waveform, a picture with captions)
and O2 (text or a link: a headline over a picture, the source as a card, a list) have nothing to
place: there is no face and no region. Their layouts are platform-zones' patterns
(`platform-zones/references/layouts.md`: kinetic type, podcast clip, news, post screenshot, list),
all sketch in 16:9. In published explainers the captions stand lower than beside a face, their
middle at 74 % of the height (240 Shorts, 41 videos).

## From 9:16 to 16:9 (sketch)

| in 9:16 | in 16:9 |
| --- | --- |
| two areas one above the other | side by side, two thirds to one third, the main thing on the left |
| circle left above the captions, 22 % of the width | circle top right in the safe box, 20 % of the height |
| hook text on the chest, or a card in the wide stretch | hook as big lines beside the person |
| captions in one line of about three words, at most as wide as `centred` | one line at the bottom, or big words in the free area |
| a wide source as a crop, or shortly as a band | a wide source fills the frame |
| an upright source fills the frame | an upright source in the middle, the sides ground, captions over it; or as a tall panel on a third, text beside it: weak as a video (measured, above). The middle is offered since 2026-11-18 and was not looked at as a video |

## Where the solver changes its mind (computed)

From `tools/sweep.mjs`: 16,128 made-up faces (15–55 % of the source's height, the eyes at 25–60 %
of it), the usual platforms of each frame, the defaults and weights of 2026-11-17 (captions in
77 px type, a hook as text first). They say what the solver does, not what looks good; a platform
named or a weight changed moves them.

- **Upright into 9:16.** Unchanged while the hairline is no higher than 13 % of the height, the
  chin no lower than 75 %, the right cheek not past 82 % of the width: with a face up to 25 % of
  the height always. One caption line stays below the chin while the chin is above about 65 % of
  the height (60 % for two lines), which agrees in direction with the 67 % at which published
  captions leave that place. Else it goes above the head, which needs the hairline at 21 % (one
  line) or 26 % (two). A hook as text stands below the chin in 54 % of the cells and above the
  head in 46 %. A zoom of 1.02–1.29× where the face only just leaves the safe box. From a face
  55 % tall (one caption line or a hook), 50 % (two lines), 40 % (a hook and two lines) only a
  smaller picture passes.
- **Wide into 9:16.** From 1080 lines the frame is filled (1.78×, soft) in 67 % of the cells
  with one caption line, 60 % with two, 49 % with a hook and two; a band stands in the rest.
  From 720 lines filling is forbidden (2.67×): the 4:5 band, then the
  square, then the whole picture as a strip.
- **Upright into 16:9.** The panel in 78–83 % of the cells; a 16:9 crop (1.78×, a third of the take's
  height) only for a face of 15–20 %. Nobody judged that crop against the panel.
- **Wide into 16:9.** Unchanged in 88 % of the cells with captions, 80 % with a hook. Captions at
  the bottom on the middle while the chin is above about 77 % of the height (72 % for two lines),
  else in a column beside the face. A hook above the head from a hairline at 27 %, else beside.
- **Into 4:5 and 1:1.** An upright take fills the frame with one caption line up to a face of
  35–45 % of its height, with a hook and two lines up to 20–30 %; above that a band down the frame
  or the whole take smaller. A wide take fills it up to 50 % (one line) and 30–40 % (a hook and
  two lines). An upright take with a face half its height takes, with a hook and two caption
  lines, an inset below half its size, the face about a fifth of the frame tall (sweep of
  2026-11-18).
