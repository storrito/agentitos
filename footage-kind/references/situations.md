# Situations

What a recording can be, told from its files' kinds (`look`) and from what its sheet shows. The
id is what you name it by; the last column is the skill of this collection that makes a video of
it today. "none yet" means no skill does: say so, and say what the nearest one would make of it.

A situation is one recording, or all the files together (P4, O2, O3, F1). The format the video
goes to is not part of it: the same situation is laid out for 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9.

## What `look` cannot tell

- A webcam before a calm or blurred background from a screen: both stand on most of their area. The sheet tells at a glance.
- Speech from sung music: both pause. transcribe tells, and how many speak.
- Who is in a camera's picture, and how many: it has no eyes for faces.
- Burned-in captions, logos, watermarks: it reads no text in the picture. A strip that never moves is its only hint.
- Files without sound that belong to one recording: the same length is all it has.
