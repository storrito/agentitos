# Followers

Reach is not followers. The pilot's biggest hit, about fifty times its median, brought almost none: people follow
a *reason* to come back, not a single good video.

## Profile

- A bio that promises what comes and how often (pattern: "The strangest <topic> on Earth · new every day").
- Pin the three strongest posts; re-pin when a new post beats the weakest pinned one (done by hand in the app).

## Series and formats

- **Numbered series per lane** ("<Series> #12") on the cover and caption line 1. In the TikTok title put the
  search term and the hook first and the series label last. *Tendency:* the label-first title coincided with the
  pilot's drop, but three other changes landed the same day.
- **Formats with an arc**, once a week: a ranking (#3 → #1, ending on a size check of all three) or a same-day
  two-parter whose part 1 ends on a real open question. *Hypothesis* ("[PART 3/5]" shorts of large accounts hold
  their views from part to part).

## Endings (tested by day, see `measurement.md`)

| arm | pattern | pilot |
|---|---|---|
| question | "Guess the number — drop it in the comments." | few comments: 0.3–0.6 per 1,000 views across wordings, yes/no lowest (0.29); one weekly window saw 1.0 on question days (*tendency*) |
| teaser | "Follow — next up: <the next post's topic>." | ~1.7× the follower gain of question days over five days each (*tendency*) |
| share | "Send this to someone who …" | next arm (*hypothesis*): Instagram weighs sends for non-followers |

## Captions

- Instagram: exactly 5 hashtags (Instagram caps at 5 since December 2025 and says hashtags categorise, they don't
  add reach), the hook as the second line, facts and footage credits, the ending of the running arm.
- TikTok: the title is search: search term and hook first, at most ~90 characters.
- **Tagging** the official accounts of footage owners and brands: no measurable effect on the pilot (shares
  1.02 vs. 0.98 per 1,000 views); costs little. Tag only accounts you verified as official.
