# Covers

A cover works in two places: the feed (full 9:16, caption and buttons on top of it) and the **profile grid**
(TikTok crops ~3:4 from the middle, ~360 px wide on a phone). For growth the grid matters most: someone who liked a
video lands on the profile and decides in seconds whether to watch more or follow.

1. **One subject, big:** at least 40 % of the area, taken from the hook shot so the cover keeps the video's promise.
2. **Full frame**, never letterboxed: a blurred-fill frame looks half as big at 360 px.
3. **Safe area:** text only between y = 300 and 1300 px, x = 80 and 900 px (on 1080 × 1920). Above sits the app's
   header, below the caption, right the buttons; the grid cuts outside y = 240–1680.
4. **Headline: 2–5 words, one thought, capitals**, very bold, ≥ 120 px, white with a dark outline, one key word in
   the accent colour. The **same words as the video's text hook**. It opens a curiosity gap; it doesn't repeat the
   title or give everything away.
5. At most **one number** as a small line under the headline, sourced in the brief.
6. **Recognisable:** always the same layout (brand or series pill on top, headline in the upper third, subject
   below), so the grid reads as a series.
7. **Contrast before beauty:** brighten dark frames, a dark gradient behind the text; it must read on a phone at 30 %
   brightness.
8. No emojis, no burned-in text or logos of the source, no clickbait arrows or circles.
9. **Check it at 360 px.** If the headline doesn't read in a second, shorten it.
