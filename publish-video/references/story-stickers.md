# publish-video: Instagram's stickers on a Story

`--stickers stickers/<name>/layer.html` puts Instagram's own stickers (a link, a poll, …) over an Instagram Story's video: the `layer.html` that agentitos' story-stickers wrote and the person saw. Only the Story targets get it.

- A Story target needs `--stickers` or `--no-stickers`: ask the person which sticker fits (a link when there is a page to go to; none is an answer too) before you publish.
- Never draw stickers into the video for this.
