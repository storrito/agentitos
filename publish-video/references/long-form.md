# publish-video: long-form on YouTube

A long-form video on YouTube wants, besides the texts from video-descriptions:

- **a thumbnail**: offer to make one with agentitos' thumbnail-and-intro (then `publish … --thumbnail <image>`), or let YouTube pick a frame;
- **chapters** in the description (`0:00 …` from `words.txt`) when the video has parts;
- the **tags**: they stay in `descriptions.json` but do not go through Storrito; say they are added in YouTube Studio.
