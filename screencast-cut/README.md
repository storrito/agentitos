# screencast-cut

Cuts a screencast recorded from **several sources at the same time** (the screen, the webcam, a phone's screen or camera, the microphone: what [rragent](https://github.com/storrito/rragent)'s screencast recorder writes) into **one video that shows where the action is**. The sources go on one clock, the voice is cleaned and evened out, the shot switches between screen, phone and face and zooms in on what changes, waits play as timelapses, and every result stays long enough to see. The cut is a [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com) project, so the person sees it as a preview before anything is rendered; the render is that same project.

> Agents: start with [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md). It is written for rragent: the recording comes from its recorder, the words from ElevenLabs Scribe through agentitos' [transcribe](../transcribe/) skill, the preview is a project of its `hyperframes` template, the video comes from its `render` tool.

```bash
node tools/screencast.mjs new screencast/demo artifacts/recordings/2026-10-23T11-04-56   # sources on one clock, voice, activity
node tools/screencast.mjs transcribe screencast/demo                                    # the words, checked against the voice → source/brief.md
node tools/screencast.mjs propose screencast/demo                                       # every sentence → proposal.json
node tools/screencast.mjs sheets screencast/demo --proposal                             # each keep's frames → sheets/keep-NN.jpg
# write screencast/demo/cut.json (the story, per keep what must be readable); the project tool: create demo, kind hyperframes
node tools/screencast.mjs build screencast/demo --out artifacts/demo/composition         # the preview beside the chat (a slow speaker a little faster)
node tools/screencast.mjs check screencast/demo                                         # the cut as text, and what to fix
node tools/screencast.mjs at screencast/demo 0:23                                       # what plays at 0:23 of the preview
node tools/screencast.mjs frame screencast/demo 0:23                                    # its source there, with a grid and the crop
# the render tool: demo → artifacts/demo.mp4
node tools/screencast.mjs finish screencast/demo artifacts/demo.mp4                     # at −14 LUFS, and demo.srt
node --test tools/test.mjs                                                              # the pauses, the cut's text, ffmpeg's options, the face view (with layout and clips beside it); no request
```

Outside rragent the same works with any HyperFrames project folder (`--out <project>/composition`, then `hyperframes render` there) and any folder with a `recording.json` (below); `transcribe` takes the words from ElevenLabs Scribe through agentitos' [transcribe](../transcribe/) skill beside this one (or `TRANSCRIBE` naming its `tools/transcribe.mjs`).

Requirements: Node 20+, ffmpeg (4.4 or newer: a steady frame rate is asked for as the installed one takes it, `-fps_mode` from 5.1 on, else `-vsync`), the HyperFrames CLI, and OpenRouter: `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, or on a Sprite the gateway's `openrouter` connection (found at `http://sprite.internal/v1/gateway/list`; an rragent agent there has no key of its own). OpenRouter finds the face in the webcam (`google/gemini-3.8-flash`, three frames, under half a cent) and marks the captions' key words; without it the circle shows the webcam's middle. No npm dependencies. Built after [best-takes](../best-takes/): its cut points, levels, timelapses and finish, copied and adapted (agentitos' folders never import from each other); captions come from [captions](../captions/), the face view's placement from [layout](../layout/) (its `place`, found as `LAYOUT_TOOL`, else beside this skill, else `/home/sprite/skills/layout`) with the face followed by [clips](../clips/)' tracker (`FRAME_TOOL`, else beside, else `/home/sprite/skills/clips`), all called as tools.

## The recording

A folder with the sources and a `recording.json`:

```json
{"started": {"webcam": 1792746297058, "phone-screen": 1792746297195, "screen": 1792746297331},
 "devices": {"mic": "Standard", "camera": "FHD Webcam"},
 "files": ["phone-screen.webm", "screen.webm", "webcam.webm"]}
```

The sources are `screen`, `webcam`, `phone` (the phone's camera), `phone-screen` and `audio` (the microphone alone), any of them; `started` is when each began, in milliseconds. When the person paused the recording, `pauses` lists when (`[{"from": …, "to": …}]`, milliseconds of the same clock): every source paused at once and the files leave that time out, so a source started after a pause starts that much sooner on the recording's clock. The phone's camera holds a moment later, when the word reaches it; its own times are `phone-pauses`. This tool does not use them yet: after a pause the phone's camera can be off by that moment (up to a few seconds when its page missed the word), so watch its pieces after a pause.

## How it works

1. **`new`** makes every source a video the preview and the renderer play well (H.264 at a steady 30 fps, a keyframe every second so every cut seeks at once, at most 2560 px wide; a phone's screen arrives with a varying frame rate). It puts them on **one clock**: the earliest start is 0, every other source starts as much later as `recording.json` says, and since every source carries the microphone, each is laid onto the main microphone by its sound (the best correlation of their loudness envelopes within ±1.5 s, else within ±1.5 s of the main microphone's start: older recordings of rragent stamped a phone's screen at its first picture, up to a minute late; a match under 0.5 keeps the recorded start). The browser's sound has gaps in its timestamps (some 4 s in six minutes); the conversion fills them with silence, so that the words' times and what plays agree. The **voice** is the clearest microphone (a source of sound alone first), on the recording's clock, rumble and hiss filtered out (`highpass`, `afftdn`). It measures **where each picture moves**, four times a second, from the same decoding that converts it (one read of each source; all sources at once): the share of changed pixels, the box around them, and which cells of a grid changed. While it converts it prints how far each source is, every ten seconds. Last, it finds the **face** in the webcam (`tools/lib/face.mjs`: three frames to a vision model, the middle answer kept as `face` in `sources.json`): the circle and the face view sit on it, not on the picture's middle (a person off to one side, a camera set high). A build finds it when `new` could not.

   **Several recordings** (`new <dir> <folder> <folder> …`, the person stopped and recorded again): each is prepared as above into `source/session-<n>/`, then they are laid one after the other on one clock. Each source's sessions are joined without encoding again (a session without that source shows its last picture there), the voice is joined with silence where a session's is shorter, and the activity follows. The brief marks where each recording begins; every word knows its recording, and a keep never plays from one into the next without a cut.

   **Cut off and run again**, `new` goes on: each source is converted into a part file and kept with its activity counts once done, so a second run converts only what is missing (rragent's bash stops a command after a few minutes; six minutes of three sources take about three).
2. **`transcribe`** takes the words from ElevenLabs Scribe through the [transcribe](../transcribe/) skill: every word with its start and end, each checked against the voice there (squeezed into a few milliseconds, drawn over a pause, put into silence: moved to where the voice is; a stretch that stays doubtful heard again alone). Names it mishears are corrected in `cut.json`'s `"fixes"`, keeping their times. **`words`** takes words from elsewhere, each with a start and an end: the words file of rragent's `transcribe` tool (ElevenLabs Scribe v2, every word with its start and end where the sound has them, `word` for the text, sounds as `kind: event`) goes in as it is; words with one time each (start = end) are refused, since the sound cannot say which stretch of voice is which word and every cut by them is a guess. A word still squeezed is put where the voice is (best-takes' `retime`; not when the words come marked `"edges": "sound"`, as the transcribe skill writes them: their times are the sound's already), sentences written twice dropped. The voice between the words is lowered by `--duck` dB (10 by default: keyboard, mouse, the room), with 60 ms ramps. `source/brief.md` is the rules of a good screencast cut, every sentence with how much each source moves in it, and every word, numbered.
3. **`propose`** drafts `proposal.json`: every sentence a keep, `"hold": 2.5` after "posted", "there it is", a wait where a picture moves between two keeps (the phone's camera only clearly more than while speaking, as for pauses); no view, no zoom. **`sheets`** makes one image per keep (of `cut.json`, or of the proposal with `--proposal`): every video source in a row, at the keep's first, middle and last moment and where a screen changes almost whole, the screens with a grid of tenths, the times in the frames when ffmpeg can write text; it prints each keep's words and moments. The agent reads the brief, the words and the sheets and writes `cut.json`: the story, and per keep the view and the zoom it decides.
4. **`build`** plans the pieces as best-takes does (cuts in the quiet between words, 20 ms fades on every spoken piece, fillers out unless they run into the words around them (less than 0.1 s on either side: the cut would clip those), pauses over `--pause` cut where nothing moves and shown where something does, faster when long, every take at one level), plus:
   - **pauses** follow what moves in them, on any source but the webcam, whatever the keep's view: on a screen (or a phone's screen) any movement over 0.2 % of the pixels, as before; on the phone's camera only what moves more than 4 times as much as it does while the person speaks (the median of its samples in all the recording's speech, never under 0.2 %; `tools/lib/activity.mjs`' `pauseMoves`). A camera always moves a little (the hand that holds it, breathing), so by 0.2 % alone nearly every pause of it stayed as a muted, faster stretch; a camera on a stand stands still while speaking and keeps 0.2 %. The build prints the phone camera's numbers. A silent moment worth seeing that it cuts: `"pauses": "keep"` on its keep, or a keep by its time; `propose` guesses its waits by the same measure;
   - **the cut's text** (captions and `.srt`) as it plays: a word that begins a sentence once the filler or the words before it are cut out gets its capital (best-takes' rule: not after "3." or "z.B.", not a name like "iPhone", not a spelling set in `"fixes"`), and a sentence end the speech model put on a filler goes onto the word before; no word's number or time changes;
   - **a hold after every keep**: `--tail` (0.45 s) after its last word, `--end` (1.5 s) after the last keep, a keep's `"hold"` (a result to see), never into the next words of the recording;
   - **waits**: the time from the word in `"waits"` to the next keep's first word plays at ×3 to ×10 (in 3–5 s) with a badge when anything on a screen changes in at least 15 % of it (a small change counts here: an agent's chat, a progress bar), else it holds 3 s before the cut; one over 50 s plays its first and last 20 s at ×10;
   - **the view** of each keep: the screen unless the agent names another (`view`; `auto` only on request: what moves most over the keep), the same for all its pieces, a pause inside it too, so the shot does not jump; a **wait** shows the keep's `waitView`, else the screen that changes most during it, the laptop's screen when none does, never the face or an idle phone;
   - **another video** (`"show"` on a keep: a file, or `{"file", "from", "sound"}`) plays instead of the recording over the keep's whole length, its words, its pauses and its hold: whole, without the circle unless the keep says `"circle": true` (since 2026-11-19: the run of that day showed the circle's silent speaker beside the shown video's, the video in the slot a phone gets beside the circle), as large as it fits above the caption line and in the middle, an upright video and a wide one alike (with `"circle": true` beside the circle, as large as the room holds it, a tall one like a phone's screen), from `from` on at its own speed, while the keep's voice plays as cut. Its pauses longer than `--pause` are cut, since nothing of the recording is seen in them. With `sound` (0–1) its sound plays as a track of its own, measured once when it is cut and brought to the voice's level (−14 LUFS, each take's before the bus), so that `"sound": 1` is as loud as the voice (it runs under the words, so it is for music, ambience or a result whose sound is the point, not for a clip in which someone speaks: the build listens to the shown stretch once through agentitos' [transcribe](../transcribe/) skill (`transcribeFile`, at most 20 s, kept in `source/shown/heard-*.json` under the clip's content and the stretch, never read by the composition) and its line says where the clip speaks under the words, with the seconds of both, or that it speaks only where the speaker does not, or has no words; without the skill, a key, or on a failure it says the general sentence) and 0.8 about 2 dB under it, for a quiet clip as for a loud one; it runs through the voice's bus and its −2 dB true-peak ceiling: a quarter of that level under the first words, rising over the last 1.5 s of them to the level at the keep's last word, held, out over the last 0.25 s. A video that ends before the last word keeps it low and fades out at its end, and the build says so. `"sound": {"hold": 0.8}` is for a clip that speaks: silent under the words, up over 0.25 s after the last word to that level in the keep's hold (not listened to: no voice runs under the words); it needs 1 s of hold (0.25 s up, half a second at its level, 0.25 s out), and a shorter one gets no track and a sentence that says what holds it short (the keep's "hold", the shown video's end, the recording's end). A "hold" the keep sets whose voice is muted for 1 s or more (the recording goes straight on into other words there) and that has no sound of the shown video is digital silence: the build says how long, with `"sound": {"hold": …}` or `"hold": 0` as the ways out. The shown video is enlarged at most 2× of its own pixels, centred in its room past that, and the build says so from 1.25×. It is the keep's whole picture: a `"view"`, `"zoom"` or `"layout"` on the same keep is left out, and the build says so.    - **behind the shots** each piece's own screen, 320 px wide, blurred and darkened, one track for the whole cut (`tracks.backdropTrack`), so that the room a shot leaves free is not a flat colour; `"background": "plain"` for the brand's background.
5. **`check`** lists the cut as it plays and what to look at: a piece that starts inside a word (✗), one that ends right after its last word because the next words follow at once, an end without a moment's hold, a result ("posted", "there it is") without 2 s of proof after it or with the face as its proof, over 6 s without words at their own speed (a wait not listed), a source that could not be laid onto the voice, and HyperFrames' own lint.
   HyperFrames' full check (`hyperframes check <out> --timeout 5m`, 0.8.221) of the face view's builds of the public test in 9:16, 16:9 and 1:1 (2026-11-19, sampled in every face keep and in the lean) found nothing: no layout, runtime, contrast or craft finding; lint only advises splitting the circle's five rings on one track into sub-compositions, as on main, which `check` leaves out for a generated file. On a laptop it needs about 90 s; at its default limit the page's load timed out.
6. **`at`** answers a moment the person names in the preview: the piece, its keep, its time in the recording, its view and zoom, its words. **`frame`** saves that moment's source, whole, with a blue line every tenth and the shot's crop in red (`screencast/<name>/frames/`).
7. **`finish`** brings the rendered video to `--loudness` (−14 LUFS) with a gain and a limiter, the picture copied, and writes the subtitles (`.srt`).

| key | what |
| --- | --- |
| `keep[].words` | a range of word numbers from the brief, both ends included, or one number |
| `keep[].at` | instead of `words`: a stretch of the recording by its time, `"7:01.5-7:06"` (or seconds), the picture alone, muted: what happens while nothing is said |
| `keep[].view` | `screen` (the default), `phone-screen`, `phone`, `face` (or `auto`, the tool's guess by what moves) |
| `keep[].text` | how tall a capital of the smallest text that must be read is, in px of the screen (read off the sheet's grid): the build says whether it reads, and the layout skill places the box at the size that does |
| `keep[].zoom` | what must be readable: a box `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in 0–1 of the source, shown as large as the safe room allows with the source around it; or `off` (the default: the whole source), or `auto` (the tool's guess) |
| `keep[].circle` | `false`: no webcam circle while the keep plays, the shot takes its room; on a `"show"` keep the default, and `true` puts the circle beside the shown video |
| `keep[].layout` | on a screen keep: `"screen-circle"` (main's form, the circle) or `"screen-panel"` (the stack, 9:16 and 4:5), or a picture of its list (`"as before"`, `"screen-fit"`, …); on a `face` keep: the candidate of the build's face-view list to show (`"crop 1:1"`, `"inset"`, `"cover"` for the frame covered from the face as before, …); without it the first of the list. A name is of one frame's list: one the frame lacks falls back to the first with a `!` that lists the frame's names; a named picture that fails says on what and which pass; on a keep without the face view it is left out, said |
| `keep[].hold` | seconds the shot stays after its last word (default `--tail`, the last keep `--end`) |
| `keep[].label` | a short title in the frame while the keep plays |
| `keep[].waitView` | the view of a wait after this keep (`screen`, `phone-screen`, `phone`, `face`); else the screen that changes most during it |
| `keep[].hook` | `true`: a moment shown ahead that plays again in its place; its words may be in another keep too |
| `keep[].pauses` / `sounds` / `gain` | as in best-takes: `"keep"` plays its pauses / fillers as they are; dB on top of the evened-out level |
| `waits` | word numbers after which the time up to the next keep plays as a timelapse |
| `fixes` | the captions' spelling by word number; `""` drops a word from the text |
| `captions` | written by `captions`: the style and the key words, `"lines": 2` for two lines over a band as before (kept by `captions`; a cut with highlights and no style writes `{"highlight": […], "lines": 2}`, hormozi as on main, and `{"lines": 2}` alone keeps the clean subtitles); `false` for none (default: clean subtitles in one line) |
| `background` | `plain`: the brand's background behind the shots instead of the blurred screen |
| `music` | `file`, `volume` (0–1, default 0.35), `from` (seconds into the file) |
