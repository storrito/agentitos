# The quality round

On `short/<name>/sheet.jpg`, before the person is asked: stills of the composition at the hook, at each beat's start and middle, on the comparison card and at the end, row by row, the times in the tool's last line. A moment closer up: `hyperframes snapshot artifacts/<name>/composition --at <seconds>`.

Each point: what to look for, then the usual fix.

1. **The first frame.** It shows what the first sentence claims, large; no people in a studio; not dark; the hook text readable on it. → Another stretch for the first beat, another hook text.
2. **The freeze**, when the first beat has one (`short/<name>/cutout/cutout-check.jpg`, and the still at its second): the outline runs around the subject only, no person, floor, second thing or piece of background inside, nothing of the subject cut off. → Another `freeze` second, a more exact `subject`, or no `freeze`. Never out with a wrong cutout.
3. **The subject, in every beat.** Gone, tiny, cut off, a blurry detail, only floor or sky. → Move `at`, another shot, `zoom` and `pan`.
4. **Foreign text and logos** in the sharp picture: a broadcaster's logo, burnt-in subtitles, a watermark, another app's logo. → `zoom` and `pan`, another stretch, another source. Never out with it.
5. **Black bars** or a frame inside the band. → Another stretch or source.
6. **The captions.** The key words in colour are the ones that carry the story; a box holds its whole phrase (the number with its unit) and sits on the words it says; a name spelled as the script has it; no caption on burnt-in text of the source. → Other `keys`; a misheard name is the script's line, spoken again.
7. **The card.** Two rows, one unit, nothing cut off, 2.5 s or longer, no caption under it, a fitting shot behind it.
8. **Two texts at once**: the hook text and the card, the card and the handle. → Move the card onto its own sentence.
9. **The end.** The last word is not cut off, the handle stands, the last picture is not black and leads back into the first.
10. **A wrong emoji, flag or spelling** anywhere on screen.

What only a render can break is checked after it: `launch.mjs finish` levels the sound, publish-video's `check` measures the file.

Say in the message of step 7 what you fixed, in a sentence, when it changed what the person will see.
