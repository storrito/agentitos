# A day's topics

- **News first.** What happened in the channel's field in the last days: read the field's news pages and the makers' own pages with your `fetch` tool, a web request each (the sources that worked are in `artifacts/channel.md`; add one when you find it). A news topic is about a week old at most. When the news is thin, an evergreen from `artifacts/backlog.md`: an older, astonishing fact that has good footage.
- **A story, not a data sheet.** A secret, a trick, a contradiction, something that should not work. The number comes second.
- **A name everyone knows** where there is one: it goes into the first sentence.
- **Lanes.** Every topic belongs to one lane of `channel.md`. On one day at most two topics of the same lane. A lane is closed only in the weekly review, with the person: eight posts and none that did clearly better than the channel's usual.
- **The mechanism.** The one-word trick behind the aha ("robot-vs-human", "swarm", "giant-arm"). At most three posts of the same mechanism in fourteen days; a new mechanism beats a fourth of the same. It goes into the `posted` event.
- **No duplicates**, and no near ones (the same subject from another video): `recall` the `posted` events, and look at `backlog.md`.
- **The series.** Every post carries its lane's numbered label ("Robot News #12"), counted from the `posted` events of the lane just before the texts are written.
- A sequel to a video that did well stands alone: no "Remember the …?" to open; a look back in the second sentence at the earliest.

What you find goes into `artifacts/backlog.md`: the topic, its lane, the lead with its source, candidate footage, newest news on top. A topic is marked `footage seen` only after its footage was looked at (the footage skill): what a search lists is not yet a picture of the subject.

The channel's own numbers lead where there are some: a rule under "Learned" in `channel.md` outranks this page.
